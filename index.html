FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to fast before my blood test?

• Routine metabolic panels, glucose, and lipid (cholesterol) tests usually require fasting for 8–12 hours. Check your specific test instructions or consult your doctor.

Can I drink water while fasting?

• Drinking plain, unflavored water is highly recommended. It keeps you hydrated and makes it easier for the phlebotomist to find your veins. Avoid coffee, tea, juice, and soda.

What is the best time to give a sample?

• Early morning is best for fasting tests. Our lab opens at 7:00 AM.

Is home sample collection safe and accurate?

• Yes. Our trained staff collect samples at your home and bring them to the lab for testing, so accuracy stays the same
